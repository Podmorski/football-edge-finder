# Phase 3 — derived goal markets

**Zero API calls, zero downloads.** Confirmation seasons (2023-24+) untouched in
all leagues. Discovery only.

## 0. Strategy pivot

The main-market hypothesis **failed**: results-only models cannot beat the sharp
market on 1X2 or O/U 2.5 in any of our four leagues (model − market = +0.0142 to
+0.0187, every CI excluding 0). New direction: Soccer Bet prices hundreds of
**derived goal markets** by formula, all settling on `(HT home, HT away, FT home,
FT away)` — which we hold for every match since 2015. Take the sharp **pre-match**
1X2 + O/U 2.5 as *input*, build a joint half-by-half model, and test calibration
per family. Recorded in `docs/PROJECT_PLAN.md` with the new principle that sharp
pre-match odds may be model **inputs** (closing remains an accuracy reference).

**Other inconsistencies found** (recorded in `core/market_code.py`):
`0-1` is "at most 1" (a range) while `3-4` is "at least 3" (not a range) — they
cannot both be ranges; the Poluvreme/Kraj `NE` set is incomplete (only 5 of 9
HTFT outcomes); Dupla Pobeda `1`/`2` collide textually with Konačni Ishod; and
"Hendikep Pobeda" appears three times with different meanings.

**Parser** (`core/market_code.py`): full grammar, `NE` as the complement of the
whole conjunction, explicit disambiguation (a code ambiguous between a goal range
and a HT/FT pair **raises** unless a family is supplied), spaces around `&`
optional, and any parseable code accepted at runtime with family `UNLISTED`.
`FIRST_GOAL` is marked **untestable** (needs the goal timeline) and `TO_QUALIFY`
out of scope. **28 tests** cover the official definitions, the three named NE
cases, the exact-complement property over all `(hth,hta,h2,a2) ∈ 0..3⁴`, the HTFT
partition, the `1-2` disambiguation, and a catalogue round-trip.

## 2. Market-anchored half model

**L1 anchor** — least-squares fit of `(lambda, mu)` to the de-margined pre-match
1X2 and O/U 2.5 (Dixon-Coles grid, rho = −0.05, L-BFGS-B on log-rates).

Residual distribution over all 12,000+ discovery matches:
**mean 0.0118, median 0.0110, p95 0.0245, max 0.2497** — the anchor reproduces
the market closely.

**L2 half split** — first-half share regressed on `(lam+mu)` and `|lam−mu|` on
training seasons only. Mean share at ~2.6 expected goals: **0.41–0.44** (below
0.5, i.e. the second half has more goals), consistent across leagues.

**L3 game state** — second-half multipliers by half-time state (ratio estimator,
shrunk 50% toward 1.0):

| league | home lead | home level | home trail | away lead | away level | away trail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| league_one_t3 | 0.962 | 0.988 | 1.060 | 1.011 | 0.961 | 1.020 |
| ligue_2_t2 | 0.989 | 0.986 | 1.019 | 1.042 | 0.969 | 1.109 |
| bundesliga_2 | 0.987 | 0.986 | 1.036 | 1.024 | 1.010 | 1.036 |
| bundesliga_1 | 1.014 | 0.993 | 1.010 | 1.056 | 0.953 | 1.041 |

Leading and trailing sides both score faster in the second half; level games
slow slightly. Effects are modest (0.95–1.11).

**Output** — joint grid over `(h1, a1, h2, a2)`, mass 1.000000, every catalogue
market derived via the parser. Baselines: **B0** = same anchor, 50/50 split,
independent halves; **B1** = B0 with a 45/55 split.

## 5. Tools

* `run.py price-match --league L --home H --away A --odds-1x2 h d a --odds-ou25 o u`
  — fair probability, fair odds and family status for all 207 markets.
* `templates/soccerbet_prices.csv` — `date, league, home, away, market_code,
  family, soccer_bet_odds, time_recorded`. The **family column is required**:
  codes collide across families (`1` is both RESULT "home wins" and

## 7. Failures and what I tried

1. **`NE` codes inside period families failed to parse** (`INE 1`) — the rules
   list them as `NE 1` under the 1H family, so I added family-supplied periods.
2. **`I>II`/`I=II`/`I<II` failed to parse** — they are direct families, not
   grammar codes; removed from the code list.
3. **DO_NOT_BET keys did not match catalogue codes** — only 1 of 4 flagged;
   corrected the keys.
4. **Five test failures, all my own test data** — wrong expected outcomes for
   `3+`, `II2+`, `I=II`, and a wrong assumption that `2-3` is ambiguous (it is
   not: `3` is not a result token).
5. **The calibration recorded only winning rows** (`y` all 1s) — fixed to record
   every (market, match) pair with `y ∈ {0,1}`, excluding voids.
6. **`beats_b0` was inverted** — the CI is on the difference, so "beats B0"
   requires `ci_high < 0`, not `ci_low > 0`. This had made every family fail.
7. **`final_pass` was written to the CSV before the Holm block added it** —
   moved the write after.
8. **`market_code` parsed as an int** in `analyse-book`, so lookups missed —
   cast to string, and added the required `family` column.
9. **Anchor solving was far too slow** with Nelder-Mead restarts — switched to
   L-BFGS-B on log-rates (8.4 ms per match).
10. **Codes collide across families** — `1`, `2`, `I1`, `I2`, `II1`, `II2`,
    `1-2`, `NE 1`, `NE 2` (207 markets, only 196 distinct codes). The probability
    dict was keyed by code alone, so colliding markets shared a value; this
    contaminated the RESULT, GOAL_RANGE_* and HTFT calibrations. Caught by
    noticing that `1` printed the same `p_fair` in two families. Fixed by keying
    on `(family, code)`; HTFT's slope moved from 0.44 to 1.01 and GOAL_RANGE_FT
    dropped out of the passing set as a result.
